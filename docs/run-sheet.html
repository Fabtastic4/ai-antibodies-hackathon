The gate — and what the rejections taught us

Read from abguard's source rather than inferred from the brief. Three of our earlier assumptions were wrong, all in the direction of over-constraining.

Novelty — corrected
≥ 3 mutations
CDRH3 Levenshtein distance from trastuzumab and from every reference CDRH3. There is no 70% CDRH3 identity test — identity over the whole Fv only selects which bar applies (≥0.70 → variant, needs 3; <0.70 → de novo, needs 2).
CDRH3 — corrected
SRWGGDGFYAMDY
The IMGT 13-mer, not the 11-mer from the structure or the FACS tables' 10-mer core. abguard's own code warns that comparing against the core "would hand every design three free mutations from the flanks alone".
Length — corrected
≤ 250 per chain
Not combined. A paired Fab may be 250 + 250, so the cap only ever binds on scFv. Both the task PDF and the slides describe it as combined.
Reference DB
610,109 CDRH3s
Ships inside the wheel, IMGT-numbered, novelty gate on by default. We do not build one from the HER2 tables.
Where the six rejections actually failed

Not one failed against trastuzumab — every rejected design was 6–9 mutations away from it. All six failed against the FACS screen: 1–2 mutations from a sequence already measured among the 525,027 unique reference loops. her2_cdrh3_53 was one mutation from SRWGSSDFYAFDY.

That reframes the gate as a map rather than a hurdle. The rule forces us exactly three mutations off half a million measured sequences. A design sitting three mutations from a measured high binder is close enough to plausibly inherit binding and far enough to be eligible. That is a target to aim at, and it is the one place where holding that dataset is an advantage over teams treating novelty as an obstacle.

Immediate consequence: 70% of refold compute landed on eligible designs, 30% did not. A local Levenshtein pre-filter against the 525k table is cheap and recovers that — and now exists.

The gate is checkable without the VM — and both batches needed it

scripts/novelty_distance.py reproduces the novelty arithmetic offline, standard library only: 20 designs against 525,027 reference loops in 38 s, 20/20 agreement with abguard team-validate. Correctness hinges on one detail — reference CDRH3s are re-extracted from each table's vh_sequence, not read from the cdrh3 column, which holds the 10-mer core.

Why it mattered immediately: both committed gate runs record abguard not found in their stdout, so neither batch's eligible list came from abguard itself — the fallback compared against trastuzumab alone. Re-checked locally: 141 of 200 eligible in batch 1, 70 of 200 in batch 2, six of batch 2's designs at distance 0 — exact duplicates of a measured CDRH3. 48 of 59 batch-1 rejections and 103 of 129 batch-2 rejections are blocked by a high-class measured binder, not by a bad design: the generator is rediscovering the motifs the FACS screen already explored.

Standing caveat: our reference set is 525,027 rows and abguard's bundle is 610,109, so the offline check can only ever be optimistic. Every eligible design sits at exactly 3 or 4 edits — zero novelty margin — so an 85k-row blind spot over a population with no margin is a real submission risk. If abguard on the VM rejects anything this check passes, that gap is the first place to look.

What we are judged on, and how strong our evidence is

Three measured properties against the measured trastuzumab baseline. Teams are ranked on S-tier count first, so the goal is designs that move all three at once.

Binding affinity

measured by SPR
Evidence: moderate

min_ipae came back — Foundry's refold-score on the VM produces it, so our ranking is PAE-derived after all, not the contact-counting fallback. But trastuzumab's own KD is 0.03–0.18 nM across 8 SPR replicates, and the HER2 weights were never trained to improve affinity — only to propose binders.

Epitope preservation

competitive binding vs trastuzumab
Evidence: good

Our strongest axis. The organisers' own spec names four hotspot atoms — K593, F573, I591, P572 — and all four sit inside the 19-residue epitope we computed independently. Hotspot obedience is 0.9375 across the batch.

Thermostability

directly measured
Evidence: thin, but real now

No model in the 59-model catalog predicts it. What we gained: abguard implements the LAP Antibody Liability Reference — 13 motif rules, organiser-sanctioned, free per design — plus framework germline-likeness. stab-ddg remains a binding ddG, usable only as a VH–VL interface proxy.

Sall three improved — the only tier that really counts
Atwo of three
Bone of three
—ties and regressions count as nothing

The honest problem, worth saying in the pitch before a judge asks: beating 0.03 nM is hard, and nothing we ran was trained to improve affinity. Our credible S-tier route is not "tighter binding by luck" — it is picking designs that hold the epitope and carry no design-specific liability, and saying plainly which axis is a proxy. Judgment is 40%, and it rewards knowing that.
