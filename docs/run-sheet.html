The eligibility window

The single most expensive thing to get wrong. A design outside this window is rejected before it is ever measured, and the track is assigned by abguard — never chosen by us, never reclassified.

Format — new
Fab · scFv
The Apheris slides say an optimisation design must be the same format as trastuzumab, so not VHH. The task PDF never said this. De novo keeps all three.
Total length
≤ 250 aa
One scFv or VHH, or VH + VL combined. immunum strips tags and linkers first.
Cysteines
No unpaired
A hard gate, so it is useless as a ranking signal — every candidate that reaches the list has passed it.
Numbering
≥ 0.1 confidence
"Is it an antibody?", via immunum. ANARCII's score is a different number and is not a substitute.
CDRH3 identity to trastuzumab — optimisation track
Rejected ≥ 70%Eligible < 70%
100% identical70% threshold0%

Trastuzumab's CDRH3 is 11 residues — WGGDGFYAMDY. Clearing 70% therefore needs at least 4 mutations (7 of 11 identical = 63.6%).
● The second bar is now sourced. The Apheris slides, presented today and newer than the task PDF, say ≥2 mutations away from every sequence in the provided data — siding with the looser of the PDF's two contradictory readings. And "the provided data" is not a short reference list: per Leonardo in Slack, our CDRH3 must differ from all 525,063 measured loops in the HER2 tables (524,346 FACS + 768 SPR). The novelty pre-check has to load that table.

What we are judged on, and how strong our evidence is

Adaptyv measures three properties against the measured trastuzumab baseline. Teams are ranked on S-tier count first — so the goal is designs that plausibly move all three at once, not a long list that each move one.

Binding affinity

measured by SPR
Evidence: moderate

ipSAE turned out to be uncomputable on this platform — no model returns a PAE matrix. We rank on openfold3 ipTM plus locally counted cross-chain contacts, pTM and interface ΔSASA. None of those is a KD.

Epitope preservation

competitive binding vs trastuzumab
Evidence: good

Our strongest axis: a cross-checked reference set and overlap scoring that costs nothing to compute. But it is not free — rewriting CDRH3 is exactly the change most likely to move the epitope, so every candidate gets screened.

Thermostability

directly measured
Evidence: weak

Nothing in the 59-model catalog predicts it. stab-ddg is a binding ddG, not folding — but since optimisation designs are now Fab/scFv, it applies to all of them as a VH–VL interface ΔΔG, a real determinant of Tm. Plus framework germline-likeness, free and local.

Sall three improved — the only tier that really counts
Atwo of three
Bone of three
—ties and regressions count as nothing

Saying plainly that axis 3 is a proxy is worth more than a confident fake number: Judgment is 40% of the pitch score, and it rewards knowing what we do not know.
