The task, and the constraint that shapes everything

Optimisation track: improve trastuzumab against HER2, submitted as VHH, scFv or Fab rather than full IgG. The organisers' her2-novel-2026-11-09 weight redesigns the CDRH3 loop only — framework sequence adherence is 1.0, so all our designs share trastuzumab's framework and differ in one 13-residue loop.

Eligibility is decided by abguard, not by us. Whole-Fv identity only selects which bar applies: at ≥ 0.70 identity a design is classified a trastuzumab variant and needs CDRH3 Levenshtein ≥ 3 from trastuzumab and from every reference CDRH3; below 0.70 it is de novo and needs 2. A design that misses its track's bar is rejected, never reclassified.

The reference set is the point. abguard ships 610,109 CDRH3s in its wheel; the two tables we were shipped hold 525,027 unique loops after IMGT extraction. Those loops densely cover the neighbourhood around trastuzumab's CDRH3 — which is exactly the neighbourhood a HER2 fine-tune samples from.

Campaign funnel

Where the 400 designs stand after each gate.

The constraint is the fourth bar. score/rank.py exits 3 and writes nothing. What the rejections have in common

The six rejections are not malformed or low-confidence designs. Zero hard errors were raised across all 20 — no format, length, alphabet, cysteine or immunum numbering failure. All six died on novelty, and in five cases the nearest measured neighbour is a CDRH3 the FACS screen rated high.

The same holds at scale: 48 of 59 rejections in batch 1 and 103 of 129 in batch 2 are blocked by a high-class binder. The generator is not producing junk — it is rediscovering the motifs the FACS screen already explored, which is what a HER2 fine-tune is built to do and precisely what the novelty gate exists to exclude.

The gate is now checkable without the VM

scripts/novelty_distance.py reproduces the novelty arithmetic offline, standard library only: 20 designs against 525,027 references in 38 s, with 20/20 agreement against abguard team-validate. Across both full batches its verdicts also match the committed eligible lists — batch 1's 140 rows are a strict subset of our 141; batch 2 agrees at 70 with one design differing each way.

Correctness hinges on one detail: reference CDRH3s are re-extracted from each table's vh_sequence (the segment between the conserved Cys and the WGxG motif), not read from the cdrh3 column, which holds the 10-mer core. Comparing against cores hands every design three free mutations from the flanks and passes designs abguard rejects.

Affinity decays with distance from the parent

All 524,313 FACS-screened variants, binned by CDRH3 core edit distance from wild-type trastuzumab and split by measured binding class.

high medium low counts above each bar

At core d3, 1,341 of 1,490 measured variants (90.0%) are high binders. By d9–d10 the distribution has inverted. Affinity is strongly conserved near the parent loop and degrades as you leave it.

The two constraints therefore oppose each other. Affinity rewards staying close to trastuzumab; the novelty gate forbids being close to anything already measured, and the measured set is densest exactly where affinity is best. This is the whole design problem in one figure, and it is the argument for seeding from measured high binders at the gate distance rather than sampling the loop freely — the 1,341 high binders at d3 are the pool to work from.

What the measured affinity ceiling looks like

182 unique CDRH3 variants with quantified SPR KD, on a log axis.

The median variant sits at 86 nM against wild type's 0.0285 nM — roughly 3,000× weaker. CDRH3 substitution is overwhelmingly destructive, and the tail runs to 13,863 nM.

The baseline is not one number. Trastuzumab itself appears eight times in the SPR table, from 0.0285 to 0.178 nM — a 6.2× replicate spread. Since a design is tiered on whether it improves on trastuzumab, which replicate the comparison uses moves the hit rate by nearly an order of magnitude: 1 of 182 variants beat the best replicate, 4 beat the mean, 7 beat the worst. Worth asking an organiser which baseline they score against.

But the left edge matters more than the median. WGSQGFYAYD is measured at 0.0234 nM, tighter than wild type, and it is 3 core substitutions away (G3S, D4Q, M9Y). A better-than-parent loop exists, is already measured, and sits exactly at the gate distance. Designs seeded 3 edits off it are eligible by construction against that sequence and need re-checking only against the rest of the reference set.

Which positions the generator moves

Per-position retention of wild-type SRWGGDGFYAMDY across the 20 refolded designs.

invariant (20/20) mostly retained mostly rewritten

Positions 1, 2, 12 and 13 are invariant — the SR…DY frame. The weight varies nine of the ten core positions and rewrites the 4–8 stretch almost completely; position 6 loses the wild-type residue in all 20. This is consistent with the 14:15 finding that pushing for higher mutation counts starts deleting paratope contacts: the constraint worth imposing is which positions move, not how many.

One substitution is worth reporting on its own. Position 11 is M in wild type, and 16 of 20 designs replace it with F. M is the MetOx motif in abguard's LAP liability reference — a methionine oxidation liability. Nothing in our generation spec asked for this, so it belongs in the pitch as an observed property of the output, not as a claimed optimisation. It is also one of only three motifs (Ngly, MetOx, FragM) that discriminate between our designs at all; the rest are inherited framework and identical across the batch.

Three premises we had wrong, corrected against the installed wheel

Read out of the code rather than the slides. All three had been constraining us in the direction that costs affinity.

1. There is no 70% CDRH3-identity test

Whole-Fv identity only selects which bar applies (≥ 0.70 → 3 mutations, < 0.70 → 2). The gate is a Levenshtein count, not an identity threshold. Our earlier "≥ 4 residues" came from arithmetic abguard never performs — a full mutation of over-constraint.

2. The IMGT CDRH3 is the 13-mer SRWGGDGFYAMDY

Not the 11-mer from the structure, and not the assay tables' 10-mer core WGGDGFYAMD. The code warns explicitly that comparing against the cores hands every design three free mutations from the flanks. This is now defended against in code, not only in prose.

3. MAX_SEQUENCE_LENGTH = 250 is per chain

It is a column check applied to heavy_sequence and light_sequence separately, so a paired Fab may be 250 + 250. The cap only ever binds on scFv. A constraint we had been designing against does not exist.

Scoring rubric

Only a fifth of the score is execution. Judgment is double that, and user fit plus path to value together equal it.

  • Judgment — 40%
    Why this choice, and on what evidence?
  • Execution — 20%
    Does it run, and reproduce?
  • User fit — 20%
  • Path to value — 20%

The novelty–affinity trade-off, the measured ceiling and the seeding argument above are all judgment-axis material. That is the case for putting them in the pitch rather than a list of ipTM values.
