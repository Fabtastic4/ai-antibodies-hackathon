Where designs reliably clear the gate.

Three measurements decide the score, each against trastuzumab measured in the same lab. But nothing gets measured unless abguard passes it, and abguard counts mutations against 610 thousand known CDRH3s. At three or four mutations, zero of twelve passed. Six or more, 79 to 89 percent. So the gate pushes every design a long way from trastuzumab. Hold that thought.

So we played the board

Axis 1 is not winnable here. Axes 2 and 3 are.

We optimised epitope preservation and stability, and we do not claim an affinity win we cannot support.

Before designing anything we asked what is even achievable. 190 variants of this exact loop with measured SPR. Affinity falls off hard with mutation distance. In the band the novelty gate forces us into, five to seven substitutions, seventy-nine measured variants, not one beats wild-type trastuzumab. Our own affinity model, running on the VM right now, says the same about our twenty. So we stopped pretending axis one was winnable and played axes two and three. If another team tells you they beat trastuzumab on affinity tonight, ask them this question.

What the evidence changed

Twice, a measurement overturned a number we liked

The proxy said everything was fine

18 tied

at the same top score of 0.75

Our paratope proxy could not separate them, so the draft order fell through to mutation count. Then the Boltz-2 co-folds landed: 8 of 18 put the antibody somewhere else on HER2 entirely — hundreds of contacts, none on the epitope. Six of those eight were designs the proxy rated best. They are demoted to ranks 13–20 and labelled, not hidden.

And we could not just rank on ipTM

r = 0.15

OpenFold3 vs Boltz-2 agreement

We scored all 210 eligible designs with OpenFold3 and 139 with Boltz-2 as well. Boltz-2 reads 0.13 ipTM higher on average, but the rank orders are close to unrelated — not an offset you can subtract. An ipTM ranking inherits whichever model produced it, so we ranked on where the antibody actually lands and kept ipTM as a cross-check.

Both results made our list look worse, and both are in the repo with the code that produced them.

Twice a measurement overturned a number we liked. First, our paratope proxy gave eighteen designs the same top score; the co-folds showed eight of them bind off the epitope, including six the proxy rated best. Second, we wanted to rank on ipTM, so we ran two structure models over every eligible design: they barely agree, correlation 0.15. Both results made our list look worse. Both are in the repo.

Our best bet

Rank 1 — six CDRH3 mutations, and the only design whose co-fold reaches more of the epitope than wild-type VH does: 11 of 19 against 8.

Our judgment call

The novelty gate forces every eligible design into a band where no measured variant has ever beaten trastuzumab's affinity. So we optimised epitope preservation and stability instead of claiming an affinity win.

Our limits

Two structure models barely agree on binding (r = 0.15), thermostability rests on a labelled proxy, and eight of our twenty co-fold off the epitope. All three are on the face of the submission file.
